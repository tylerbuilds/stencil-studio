# Make a social design

Choose **New design** to start with an empty 1080 × 1080 square. Give it a title, choose a background and press **Create design**. The size picker offers 21 presets for posts, stories, thumbnails, covers, print and slides. You can also enter a custom width and height between 64 and 4096 pixels.

If you are deciding between sizes, use the [canvas size guide](research/social-sizes.md). It explains which choices are working sizes and which come from a platform's published guidance. Check the upload preview in the app you will post to.

## Choose a layout or build your own

Open **Templates** to browse 24 editable layouts. Choose **Posts & carousels**, **Stories**, **Thumbnails & covers** or **Print & slides**, then search within that type. Try `photo`, `quote`, `craft` or `carousel`. Clear the search or choose **All templates** to widen the results. Selecting a template opens a new design; your previous edited design is kept in **My designs**.

For a blank canvas, open **Elements** and add text, shapes or frames. Select a layer to change its colour, position and size. Use **Draw** for a freehand stroke. Each stroke remains a layer you can move or resize. Use the layers list to select artwork and change what sits in front.

Photo templates contain empty frames. Select a frame and choose **Choose image**, then pick a photo from your device. Use **Crop & position** to move the photo within the frame. Change the words, colours and other layers separately. **Add frame** and **Add collage** let you build a photo layout on a blank canvas too.

The **A photo walk** template starts with five pages: Cover, Colour, Details, Light and Your turn. Replace its photos and advice, then use the page strip to review the sequence. **Move earlier**, **Move later** and **Position in design** change the order.

## Make copies in other sizes

With the page you want to reuse selected, choose **Make size copies**. Select the sizes and press **Create copies**. The original page stays in place and the copies are added as editable pages.

**Scale the layout** is selected by default. Review each copy: a square composition may need a new photo crop, shorter text or different spacing when made tall or wide. Adjust those layers before downloading.

On a 1080 × 1920 page, **Guides & rulers** offers **Pinterest text area**. Choose **Add text-area guides** to mark its published boundaries. The guides can be edited and stay out of downloads. This text area belongs to Pinterest video Pins; check a separate upload preview when using the artwork elsewhere.

## Download the finished images

Open **Download → Post images**. Choose **Current page** for one image, or **All pages · ZIP file** for numbered images in the design's current page order. Each image keeps that page's pixel size.

- **PNG** suits graphics and text. Select **Transparent PNG background** when you want the page background removed.
- **JPG** can make photo designs smaller. It keeps a painted background and cannot be transparent.

Press **Download image** or **Download ZIP**. For a carousel, unzip the download and upload the numbered images in order. Review the crop, text and sequence in the platform's composer before publishing. If you only want one page from a larger design, select it first and choose **Current page**.

A design can hold up to **30 pages**. One image archive allows up to **120 million total pixels** and **64 MiB**, including the ZIP contents. If the archive is too large, use JPG, make smaller pages or download individual pages. **Cancel download** stops an archive before a file is saved.

## Keep an editable copy

Use **File → Save design** to keep a named design on this device. Open **My designs** to find it later. Use **File → Save JSON** for an editable file you can keep elsewhere or move to another device, then **File → Open JSON** to continue working on it. PNG and JPG are finished images; keep the JSON when you want to edit the layers again.

An agent can also create a native Stencil design from a structured request. Follow [Make editable designs from an agent](agent-design.md), open its JSON in Stencil, and review the words, images and layout before downloading.
