# Choose a canvas for your post

Start with the post, thumbnail or cover you want to make. The sizes below are in pixels. Add your content, download it, then check the platform's upload preview before publishing. A canvas size does not decide file limits, available account features or how a design appears on every screen.

Checked against official guidance on **2026-10-10**. “Working size” means a useful Stencil canvas within the cited guidance. “Recommended” means the platform publishes those dimensions for that particular surface.

The portrait and landscape post choices use the LinkedIn organic photo guide's accepted ratio range. They are also useful general canvases; their names do not promise suitability for every app. Page covers, personal profile banners and advertising placements are separate surfaces.

## Keep important text visible

The Pinterest video Pin guide applies that page's published margins to its 1080 × 1920 canvas: left 65, top 270, right 195 and bottom 790 pixels. It is a Pinterest guide, so check a separate preview when reusing the design elsewhere.

Keep the main message near the centre of covers and check it on the screens you use. YouTube publishes a text area at its minimum banner dimensions; Stencil does not scale that into a universal guide for the larger preset. X also explains that header trimming varies by screen and browser.

## Reels, TikTok and advertising

The portrait video canvas follows 9:16. [TikTok's June 2026 Non-Spark ad guide](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-auction-in-feed-ads?lang=en) supports that shape, but its requirements and overlays concern advertising. [X's ad specifications](https://help.x.com/en/business-and-advertising/creative-ad-specifications) likewise vary by placement.

The available search-index copy for [Meta Reels advertising](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads) describes 9:16, but was crawled last year. Direct access required login. The [Instagram photo help page](https://help.instagram.com/1631821640426723) was unavailable during this check, so these presets make no current organic Instagram photo claim and do not add an Instagram-specific 3:4 choice.

## Other starting sizes

The existing choices keep their names and dimensions so older designs continue to open: Poster 720 × 960, Landscape card 1000 × 600, Presentation 1920 × 1080, Web banner 1500 × 500, A4 CV and A4 menu 794 × 1123. The A4 choices have an A4-shaped pixel canvas; choose the physical paper size and check scaling when printing.

You can also enter a custom canvas from 64 to 4096 pixels on each side.
